IB Film Assessments

Everything you need to understand the four IB Film components.

Film Portfolio

Three film elements explored through practical work and reflection.

  • Choose elements such as editing, sound, mise-en-scène.
  • Create short practical pieces demonstrating each element.
  • Write reflective statements linking practice to theory.

Textual Analysis

A close analysis of a chosen scene from a prescribed film.

  • Focus on film language and meaning.
  • Use terminology precisely.
  • Structure your essay clearly.

Collaborative Film Project

Work as a group to create an original film.

  • Assign roles and responsibilities.
  • Document collaboration and decision-making.
  • Reflect on process and final product.

Comparative Study

A comparison of two films from different contexts.

  • Identify clear points of comparison.
  • Integrate context, theory, and analysis.
  • Present visually (slides or similar).

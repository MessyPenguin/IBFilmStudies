Welcome to IB Film Studies

This site is your hub for IB Film at Sixth Form: theory, analysis, production, assessments, and curated resources. It mirrors the structure of the Computing site you already use, but focuses entirely on film.

  • Use Theory to build your film language.
  • Use Analysis to practise textual analysis and essay skills.
  • Use Production to support your practical work and portfolio.

  • Use Videos, Quizzes, and Flashcards for revision and independent study.
